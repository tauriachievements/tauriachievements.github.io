How the shuffle works

The Rated BG Picker splits everyone who turned up into two teams of 10. When more players are ticked than fit, the rest sit out in turns, match after match. These are all the rules it follows.

Picking Before the shuffle

Tick everyone who is there and set what each team needs. Shuffle turns on once the numbers work.

  • Tick any number of players. Each one plays the spec the list shows for them: healer specs are healers, ranged damage specs are ranged, and everyone else is melee, tanks included.
  • Each team sets how many melee (1 to 5), ranged (1 to 5) and healers (1 to 4) every team gets. It starts at 4 melee, 3 ranged and 3 healers, and the three must add up to 10.
  • Shuffle turns on once every group has enough ticked players for both teams. Until then the bar says what is missing, such as "tick 2 more ranged". After that it says how many will sit out.

Alts and second specs

  • A character listed with two specs has a row for each, and only one of them can be ticked. Ticking the other spec switches to it.
  • All of one player's characters can be ticked, and at most one of them plays a match. The shuffle picks the character that keeps the most flag carriers playing, then the one that gives the best bench under the rules below.
  • Turns on the bench belong to the player, not the character. Someone who sat out on one character plays first on any of them, and sits out only once a match.

Bench Who sits out

Melee, ranged and healers each bench their own extra players. Of all the ways to do that, the shuffle takes the one that does best on these rules, checked in this order:

  1. Two flag carriers play. At least two players marked FC play every match, or every marked player when fewer than two are marked. Beyond those two, flag carriers sit out like anyone else.
  2. Nobody sits out twice in a row if it can be avoided. Whoever sat out the last match plays the next one. When more than half of a group has to sit out, some sit out again, and those who have sat out the most matches in a row play first.
  3. Classes stay even. When a group has two or more players of a class, an even number of them plays, so both teams get the same count. Rather than split 3 Hunters 2–1, the third Hunter sits out. Where that can't work for every class, as few classes as possible end up uneven.
  4. Turns are shared. The players who have sat out the fewest matches so far sit out next.
  5. Late arrivals play. Someone ticked after the last match plays before someone who played it.
  6. The rest is random.

Good to know

  • The bench is listed under the two teams. After the next shuffle, players back from the bench have a green arrow and stripe, and their team's heading counts them.
  • Shuffle again and Change players keep everyone's turns. Clear starts them over.

Teams Who plays on which team

Each team gets exactly the melee, ranged and healers set under Each team, and every group is split as evenly as it can be.

  • Each group is split on its own: healers, ranged and melee.
  • Within a group, each class is split evenly: 4 Death Knights go 2 and 2.
  • Within a class, each spec is split evenly: an Arms and a Fury Warrior go one to each team.
  • Which team gets the odd one out of a class or spec, and who plays on which side, is random.

Flag carriers

  • Marking flag carriers is optional. The FC button marks any ticked player, before or after the shuffle.
  • At least two of them play every match. From three marked on, they take turns on the bench like everyone else, so when only a few are marked they sit out less often than the rest of their group.
  • The flag carriers who play are split evenly: one per team with two, 2–1 with three.
  • The marks stay through Shuffle again and Change players. Unticking a player drops their mark.

Not balanced

  • Item level and traits are shown, but the shuffle doesn't use them.
  • A class isn't balanced across groups: a Restoration and a Feral Druid can land on the same team.

Playing After the shuffle

Each team has a leader who invites the others, and the next match is one click away.

  • The group leader has a gold crown and sends the invites. The shuffle crowns each team's first healer listed, or its first player when it has no healer. Click any other player on the team to move the crown.
  • Copy invites copies an /inv Name-Realm line for everyone on the team except the leader, ready to paste into a macro.
  • Shuffle again plays the next match with the same players, and crowns new leaders.
  • Change players unlocks the list, to tick or untick players between matches.
  • Clear unticks everyone and starts the turns over.

Sharing

  • The page's address always holds the whole picker: the ticks, the Each team numbers, the flag carriers, both teams and their leaders, the bench and everyone's turns.
  • Send it to someone and they see exactly what you see. Their Shuffle again follows the same turns as yours.
  • A player who has left the player list since is left out, and the page says so.
